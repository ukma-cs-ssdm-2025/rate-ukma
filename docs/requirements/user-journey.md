# Task 3.1 — User Journey (Happy Path)

**Persona:** "The pre-registration planner with no one to ask": a second-year bachelor planning their third year (Profile 1).

**Goal:** Before March registration opens, turn a list of 15–20 unfamiliar courses into a single plan: a main pick for each slot, backups marked for limited or risky courses, credits and hours that add up, and a clear idea of each course's real workload. The student should come away feeling "I chose, not guessed."

**Journey:**

| # | Step (user action) | Goal / expected result | Related pain/gain (from VPC) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Signs in** to Rate UKMA with their university account, one to two weeks before registration | Gets access to ratings left only by students who actually took each course | Pain: "I don't know anyone who took it" → Gain: honest signal from verified students |
| 2 | **Filters** the catalog by specialty, semester and rating | Narrows the whole catalog to a short list of candidates, including good courses they hadn't heard of | Job: compare 15–20 courses · Gain (unexpected): discovering a good course they didn't know existed |
| 3 | **Scans** the course map (usefulness × difficulty) and drops the "hard and useless" candidates | Sees many courses on one screen, and the riskiest picks drop out early | Pain: "stuck for a semester with a bad choice" · Pain: "20 tabs, losing track" · Gain: courses side by side |
| 4 | **Reads** ratings and reviews on each remaining course page, focusing on reviews about the current teacher | Learns the real workload and usefulness of each course from people who took it | Key job: "how much time will it actually take" · Pain: reviews about a different teacher · Gain (required): honest workload signal |
| 5 | **Adds** the chosen courses to their plan in the ІНП planner and **marks** the limited or risky ones' alternatives as "backup" | One plan holds both the main picks and the backups, so a course that fills up in seconds or gets cancelled isn't a dead end | Key job: main pick + backup per slot · Pain: "the course fills up in seconds" · Pain: "20 tabs, losing track" |
| 6 | **Confirms** in the ІНП planner that credits and hours add up | **Core value:** goes into registration with a ready plan instead of a guess, with no surprises inside САЗ | Gain (required): shortlist with backups ready before registration · Gain: credits checked automatically · Pain: "over the hour limit discovered in САЗ" · Emotional job: "I chose, not guessed" |
