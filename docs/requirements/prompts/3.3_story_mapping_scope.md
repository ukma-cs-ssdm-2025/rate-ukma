# Task 3.3 — Story Mapping & MVP Scope

Inputs:
- Story pool and map from task 3.2, section 2
- Journey: `docs/product-discovery/results/user-journey.md`, section 1
- Risks and team decisions: `docs/product-discovery/results/critical-analysis.md`

Context: the team must build and demo the MVP live by the end of the course. The ІНП planner milestone is 1 December.

Task: draw one clear MVP cut line through the story map.

Rules:
1. `live` stories are the existing baseline, NOT semester work. List them separately as "Already live".
2. MVP must be a thin end-to-end slice: together with "Already live", it lets the persona walk through ALL 6 journey steps in one live demo. Put the minimum per step, not the best ideas.
3. Prefer `in development` stories over `new`. A `new` story gets into MVP only if a journey step breaks without it.
4. Every Out of Scope story gets a one-line reason (not needed for the demo, too big for the semester, depends on unavailable data such as САЗ seats or the timetable, or low value now).

Output (Markdown):
- **Story map with the cut line:** journey steps as columns, then the rows "Already live", "MVP (In Scope)" and "Post-MVP (Out of Scope)".
- **In Scope table:** `ID | Step | Story`
- **Out of Scope table:** `ID | Step | Story`
